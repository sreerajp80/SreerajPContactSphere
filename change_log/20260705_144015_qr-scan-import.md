# QR scanning — import a contact by scanning a QR code

Implements [plans/20260705_143140_qr-scan-import.md](../plans/20260705_143140_qr-scan-import.md).

## What changed

- **`pubspec.yaml`** — added `mobile_scanner: ^7.0.1` (resolved to 7.2.0), the maintained
  successor to the discontinued `qr_code_scanner` that docs/known-gaps.md recommended.
- **`android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`** — declared the `CAMERA` permission with a
  comment (mobile_scanner merges its own, but the manifest keeps every permission auditable
  in one place). No runtime-permission code: the `MobileScanner` widget requests camera
  access itself and reports denial through `errorBuilder`.
- **`lib/screens/qr_scan_screen.dart`** (new) — `QrScanScreen`: full-screen camera scanner
  restricted to QR symbology, with a torch toggle (hidden when the device reports no torch,
  via `ValueListenableBuilder` on the controller state), an accent-colored aiming frame and
  hint, and an in-screen error state that gives permission denial its own wording. On the
  first non-empty detection the scanner stops and the payload is routed exactly like the
  `.vcf`-intent flow in `main.dart`:
  - not `BEGIN:VCARD` → "Not a contact QR code" snackbar, scanning resumes;
  - unparseable → "Could not read a contact from this code", scanning resumes;
  - one contact → `AddEditContactScreen` pre-filled for review (save runs the normal
    two-way sync); the scan screen pops with `true` on save, resumes otherwise;
  - several contacts → confirm dialog, bulk import via `ContactSyncService.saveContact`,
    pop with `true` when at least one saved.
  App-lifecycle pause/resume of the camera is handled by the widget itself
  (`useAppLifecycleState` defaults to true in mobile_scanner 7.x).
- **`lib/screens/contact_list_screen.dart`** — new overflow-menu item **"Scan QR code"**
  (grouped after the vCard import/export entries) with a `scan_qr` menu case that pushes
  `QrScanScreen` and reloads the list when it pops `true`.
- **`docs/known-gaps.md`** — QR bullet updated: share + scan now fully wired.
- **`docs/dependencies.md`** — QR line now `qr_flutter` (render/share) + `mobile_scanner`
  (camera scan → import).

## Verification

- `flutter analyze` — no issues.
- `flutter test` — all 68 tests pass (pre-existing `MissingPluginException` host-test noise
  from platform channels unchanged).
- Manual on-device pass (scan our own share dialog, a non-contact QR, and the
  permission-denied path) still recommended — no device was attached during implementation.

## Out of scope (unchanged from plan)
